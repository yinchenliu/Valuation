A blank field means "derive it". Left blank, the ratio is derived from this filing's statements at full precision, and the result page labels it derived and says how many filing-years produced it. The share price then equals the one cli.py reports for the same filing and the same market data.
A value you type is used exactly as typed, and the result page labels it supplied: nothing in this platform checks a figure you typed against the filing.
Typing the grey figure shown inside a field is not the same as leaving the field blank. That figure is the derived value rounded to one decimal place, shown so you can read it; the derivation itself is not rounded, so typing it back gives a different share price. For Walmart that rounding moved the price by about 3%.
